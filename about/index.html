About The Store

A clean storefront for your FiveM community.

This template gives your server a modern resource store on GitHub Pages, with category pages and hosted checkout links when you are ready to sell.

What It Is

Static, fast, and easy to edit

The site is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. That means it loads quickly, works well on GitHub Pages, and does not require a backend server.

View Store

What You Add

Your branding and products

Replace the placeholder resources, update the store name, connect your Discord, and paste checkout URLs from your payment provider.
